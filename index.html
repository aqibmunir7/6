Our story

Baked in the Valley since 2018

Deb's Artisan Bakehouse is a women-owned, small-batch artisan bakehouse in Middletown, Maryland — baking in the Valley since 2018.

It has been voted Frederick County Maryland's Best Bakery 7 years in a row. The pastries are hand-crafted in small batches, and they sell out — people line up before the doors open.

If you have only seen Deb's on Instagram, this page is the missing piece: the address, the hours, and what to ask for when you get there.
